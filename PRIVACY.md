# Privacy Policy — OpenClaw Gmail

Last updated: October 9, 2026

## What this app does

OpenClaw Gmail is a personal-use tool that connects the owner's self-hosted
AI assistant to the owner's Gmail account. It runs on the owner's own Mac
mini and serves only the owner.

## Data collection and use

- The app reads email metadata and message content from the owner's Gmail
  account, solely to provide requested assistant features (summarizing mail,
  finding receipts, drafting replies).
- The app may create draft emails and send emails only when the owner
  explicitly asks it to.
- Data is transmitted directly between the owner's devices and Google
  servers. No email data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for
  advertising.
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.

## Data storage

- Google OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's Mac mini with
  restricted file permissions.
- Email content is processed in memory; the app does not maintain its own
  email archive.

## Data retention and deletion

- Email data is retained only as long as needed to fulfill a request.
- Deleting the app and its local files removes all stored credentials.
